Our Services

A managed property service built to create trust and convert interest

AUROX handles property capture, listing management, featured placement, and inquiry filtering so owners can focus on results instead of coordination.

01

Property Listing Management

We upload, organize, and maintain property listings with strong descriptions, correct status, and structured media presentation.

02

Virtual Tour Production

We create polished video walkthroughs and supporting property media so users can explore remotely before scheduling a visit.

03

Inquiry Filtering

We review incoming inquiries and connect owners only with qualified prospects to reduce time waste and improve lead quality.

04

Featured Listing Promotion

Priority placement for premium listings helps high-value properties gain stronger visibility across the platform.

05

Expiry & Status Control

Listings can be managed as available, unavailable, removed, or expired, keeping the platform current and reliable.

06

Manual Monetization Support

We support listing fees, tour fees, and featured promotions with a manual payment workflow during the MVP phase.

Why It Works

A focused operating model for speed, quality, and trust

  • All listings are controlled by AUROX, which helps maintain quality.
  • Virtual-first viewing reduces unnecessary travel and shortens decision time.
  • Filtered inquiries improve owner satisfaction and lead seriousness.
  • The MVP keeps complexity low while preparing the platform for future growth.

Operational Flow

1

Property owner contacts AUROX.

2

AUROX visits and captures the media.

3

Listing is uploaded and managed internally.

4

Interested users submit inquiries through the platform.

5

AUROX filters and connects only qualified users to the owner.
